# Dataset access

The repository does not redistribute third-party datasets.

## Public datasets cited by the manuscript

- LOL: https://daooshee.github.io/BMVC2018website/
- LOL-v2: https://github.com/flyywh/CVPR-2020-Semi-Low-Light
- ExDark: https://github.com/cs-chan/Exclusively-Dark-Image-Dataset
- SID: https://github.com/cchen156/Learning-to-See-in-the-Dark

Download datasets from their respective official sources and comply with their licenses.

The expected paired-directory format is documented in `README.md`.
